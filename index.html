How to Play

Goal

Every island shows one or more target numbers. Swipe to slide every tile at once. Tiles that collide combine into one — land a tile exactly on each target before your moves run out.

Operators

Pick + − × ÷ before you swipe. Colliding number tiles combine using whichever operator is selected, in the order they meet — so the same swipe gives a different result depending what you've chosen.

Special Tiles

MirrorFlips the sign of whatever number drifts into it, no matter which operator is selected.
WildcardCopies the number that merges with it and doubles it.
FreezeA frozen island. It never moves and never merges — everything else has to work around it.

Harder Islands

Later currents show two or three targets at once, often laid out in separate rows. Every target you've hit stays checked off, even after that tile merges again — clear them all within the move limit.

Moves & Time

Each swipe costs one move, even if nothing merges. A swipe that changes nothing on the board is free. Fewer moves earns more stars, and your fastest clear is saved as that island's best time.
