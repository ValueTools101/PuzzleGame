Privacy Policy

Numerix Drift · Last updated 2026-09-03

Short version: Numerix Drift does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything you do in the game — level progress, stars, best times, daily-challenge results — stays only on your own device, in your browser's local storage. There are no accounts, no servers, and nothing is ever sent to us.

What the app stores, and where

Numerix Drift saves your progress using your device's local browser storage (localStorage), which never leaves your device:

  • Which levels you've unlocked and completed, and your star rating for each
  • Your best completion time per level
  • Your results on past Daily Drift challenges

This data is not personal information — it isn't linked to your name, email, or any identifier — and it is never transmitted anywhere. Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser's site data for Numerix Drift permanently deletes it.

Third-party services

The app loads two typefaces (Fraunces, Manrope, JetBrains Mono) from Google Fonts at fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. Loading a font makes a standard web request to Google's servers, which may log request metadata (such as your IP address) under Google's own privacy policy. We do not control or receive that data. No other third-party service, analytics tool, or advertising SDK is used.

Data we collect

We collect none. No accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers.

Children's privacy

Numerix Drift is designed for ages 10 and up and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
